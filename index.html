CSV batch assessment

CSV headers: age,sex,tc,hdl,sbp (required), and bp_treated,smoker,diabetic (optional, 1/0 or yes/no). Maximum 2 MB / 5,000 rows. All calculations run locally; no CSV is uploaded.

Rows with invalid values are marked ERROR in the output. Batch mode assumes all rows are CVD-free eligible adults. Avoid patient identifiers in files you share.

Evidence and limitations

D'Agostino RB Sr et al. General cardiovascular risk profile for use in primary care. Circulation 2008;117:743–753. PubMed. The derivation cohort was predominantly White; clinical calibration can differ in contemporary populations. Use clinical judgment and applicable guidelines.
